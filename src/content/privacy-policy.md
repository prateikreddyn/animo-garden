# Privacy Policy for Animo

**Effective date:** October 6, 2026
**Last updated:** October 6, 2026

Animo ("Animo," "we," "us," or "our") is a gentle companion that helps you remember and take your medicines. It offers a digital garden that grows as you take your doses, simple pill scanning, and messages from family members. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Animo website at https://animo-garden.lovable.app/ and the Animo service (together, the "Service").

We know that medicine information is personal. We have tried to write this policy in plain language, and we collect only what we need to make Animo work.

**Important:** Animo never gives medical advice. It is not a substitute for your doctor or pharmacist, who always come first.

---

## 1. Information we collect

### Information you give us
- **Account information:** such as your name or nickname, and your email address or phone number, when you sign up or log in.
- **Medicine information:** the names, doses, and schedules of the medicines you add, and your confirmations that you have taken a dose (by tapping or saying "yes").
- **Pill scans:** photos or images of pills you scan so we can check that it is the right one. 
- **Voice input:** if you use voice to confirm a dose, your spoken response may be processed to understand your answer.
- **Family connections and messages:** the family members or caregivers you invite, and the messages they send to you (or you send to them) through Animo.
- **Communications with us:** anything you send us when you contact us for support or feedback.

### Information collected automatically
- **Garden and activity data:** how your garden is doing, which is based on your dose confirmations and use of the Service.
- **Device and usage information:** such as browser type, device type, operating system, IP address, language, pages viewed, and the date and time of your visits.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** small files or local storage used to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, and understand how the Service is used (see Section 7).

We do not knowingly collect information that we do not need to run the Service.

---

## 2. How we use your information

We use your information to:
- Create and manage your account and keep you signed in.
- Provide the core Service: checking pills, tracking doses, and growing your garden.
- Deliver reminders and messages from family members.
- Share dose-related updates with the family members or caregivers **you choose** to connect with (see Section 3).
- Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Service.
- Respond to your questions and support requests.
- Send you important service notices, such as changes to this policy.
- Comply with legal obligations and protect against fraud, abuse, or security threats.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use your medicine information for advertising.

---

## 3. How we share your information

We share information only in the following situations:

- **With family members or caregivers you connect.** Animo is built to let your family send you a hello. Depending on the settings you choose, people you invite may be able to see limited information, such as whether you took a dose or how your garden is doing. You control who is connected, and you can remove them at any time.
- **With service providers.** We use trusted companies that help us run the Service, such as hosting, database, authentication, image or voice processing, and analytics providers. They may access your information only to perform services for us and are required to protect it.
- **For legal reasons.** We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or a valid government request, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users, the public, or Animo.
- **In a business transfer.** If Animo is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will tell you before it becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
- **With your consent.** We will share your information in other ways if you ask us to or agree to it.

We may share aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably be used to identify you.

---

## 4. Health-related information

Information about your medicines is sensitive health-related information. We handle it with extra care: we use it only to provide the Service, we limit who can access it, and we do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Animo is not a healthcare provider, and information in Animo is not a medical record. Pill scanning is a helpful check, but it can make mistakes. **Always confirm your medicines with your pharmacist or doctor, and never change how you take a medicine because of something in Animo.**

---

## 5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. When you delete your account, we will delete or de-identify your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we are required to keep certain information for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons. Pill scan images are kept only as long as needed to perform the check and provide the Service, unless you choose to save them.

---

## 6. Your choices and rights

You can:
- **Access and update** the information in your account.
- **Delete** your account and associated data by contacting us at the address below.
- **Remove** family members or caregivers from your connections.
- **Withdraw consent** for optional features, such as voice input or camera access, through your device or browser settings.
- **Opt out** of non-essential communications.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. These may include the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11. We may need to verify your identity before acting on your request.

## 7. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and local storage to keep you logged in, remember your settings, keep the Service secure, and understand how people use Animo. You can control cookies through your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, some parts of the Service may not work properly.

---

## 8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your login details private, and contact us right away if you think your account has been accessed without your permission.

---

## 9. Children's privacy

Animo is designed for adults, especially seniors. It is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
